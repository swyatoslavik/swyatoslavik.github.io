SmartWake
Smart alarm for iPhone and WHOOP

Wake up gently. Get up for sure.

SmartWake is an iPhone alarm app. During the night it reads your heart rate from a WHOOP band over Bluetooth, picks a light moment before your alarm time to wake you, and at the alarm time rings until you solve a short task (math, typing a phrase, memory, word translation and more). After that it keeps watching your heart rate for a while to make sure you did not fall asleep again.

Your data stays yours

SmartWake has no servers and no accounts of its own. Sleep data is stored on your iPhone. If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app saves your nights to a SmartWake folder on your own Google Drive and can only see the files it created there. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Features

  • Heart rate and RR intervals from WHOOP 5.0 directly over Bluetooth, all night.
  • Gentle wake-up window before the alarm time; guaranteed ring at the alarm time via iOS AlarmKit.
  • Wake-up tasks: math, typing, memory, shaking, translation, heart rate, barcode.
  • Optional sleep sound tracking: keeps only short fragments of snoring, sleep talking and similar sounds.
  • Optional backup of nights and settings to your own Google Drive, with automatic clean-up after N days.
