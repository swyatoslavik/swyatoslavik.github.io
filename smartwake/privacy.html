Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

SmartWake (“the app”) is an iPhone alarm app. This policy explains what data the app uses and where it goes. In short: the app has no servers, no analytics and no advertising. Your data is stored on your iPhone and, only if you connect it, on your own Google Drive. The developer never receives your data.

Data the app uses

  • Heart rate and RR intervals from your WHOOP band, received over Bluetooth on your iPhone. Used to choose the wake-up moment and to check that you got up. Stored on your iPhone.
  • Alarm settings, wake-up task results and morning survey answers you enter. Stored on your iPhone.
  • Apple Health data (read only): sleep stages, heart rate variability and resting heart rate, if you allow it. Used to compare with the app’s own estimates. Stored on your iPhone. The app never writes to Apple Health.
  • Microphone, only when you turn on “Track my sleep”. Sound is analysed on the iPhone with Apple’s built-in sound classifier; only short fragments recognised as snoring, sleep talking, moaning, coughing and similar sounds are saved, everything else is discarded immediately. Fragments are stored on your iPhone.

Google Drive (optional)

If you choose “Sign in to Google and connect Drive”, the app asks Google for these permissions:

  • drive.file — create files in your Google Drive and access only the files and folders the app created. The app cannot see or change any other files on your Drive.
  • openid and email — to show which Google account is connected.

With this access the app uploads to a SmartWake folder on your Drive: your nights (one database file per night), saved sleep sound fragments and your sound labels, and your app settings. It deletes nights older than the number of days you choose (14 by default) from that folder, moves fragments you mark as false detections to a separate folder, and downloads your nights back if you reinstall the app.

The connection goes directly from your iPhone to Google. Sign-in tokens are stored in the iPhone Keychain. The developer has no access to your Google account, your Drive or the files the app creates there. You can disconnect at any time in the app (Settings → “Sign out of Google”) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

SmartWake’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to provide the backup and restore features described above, is not transferred to anyone, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, servers or cloud of its own; no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.
  • No selling, sharing or transferring of your data to third parties.
  • No network connections other than to Google sign-in and the Google Drive API, and only after you connect Drive.

Keeping and deleting data

Data on your iPhone stays until you delete it or remove the app. Nights on your Drive are deleted automatically after the period you choose; the training folder with labelled sounds is kept until you delete it yourself. You can delete the SmartWake folder on your Drive at any time.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Политика конфиденциальности

Действует с 23 ноября 2026 г.

У SmartWake нет своих серверов, аналитики и рекламы. Данные хранятся на твоём iPhone и, только если ты подключишь его, на твоём Google Диске. Разработчик твоих данных не получает.

  • Пульс и RR-интервалы с браслета WHOOP по Bluetooth: чтобы выбрать момент пробуждения и проверить подъём.
  • Настройки будильников, результаты заданий, утренний опрос.
  • «Здоровье» (только чтение): фазы сна, HRV, пульс покоя — для сверки с алгоритмом.
  • Микрофон — только при включённом «Отслеживать мой сон». Звук анализируется на телефоне; сохраняются лишь короткие фрагменты храпа, речи во сне, стонов и т. п., остальное сразу отбрасывается.

Google Диск (по желанию). Доступ drive.file: приложение создаёт папку SmartWake и видит только свои файлы, остальной Диск ему недоступен; openid и email — чтобы показать, какой аккаунт подключён. В папку выгружаются ночи, фрагменты звуков с разметкой и настройки; ночи старше выбранного срока (по умолчанию 14 дней) удаляются. Соединение идёт напрямую с телефона в Google, токены — в связке ключей iPhone. Отключить можно в приложении или на myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Использование и передача данных, полученных через Google API, соответствует Google API Services User Data Policy, включая требования Limited Use: данные нужны только для резервной копии и восстановления, никому не передаются, не используются для рекламы и не читаются людьми.
